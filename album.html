NEW ALBUM

The new album began with vocal recordings made in Thailand in late 2025. The performances were recorded in one take, with freestyle elements and a spontaneous approach to the delivery.

Rather than leaving those recordings as they were, IgoRacks returned to the original vocals and built entirely new beats around them, drawing on samples from classical music. He is producing, arranging, mixing and mastering the material himself.

The project is now close to completion. The new edition of Grover is coming soon. Its full title and release date have not yet been announced.
