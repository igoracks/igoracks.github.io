GYMNOPÉDIE NO. 1

“Gymnopédie No. 1” was created for Round 1 of the 19th Independent Rap Battle.

The track takes its title from Erik Satie's composition Gymnopédie No. 1, which IgoRacks sampled to build the instrumental for his entry.

The battle submission was presented under the title “Gymnopédie No. 1”.
