BIOGRAPHY / ARCHIVE

IgoRacks

Hip-hop artist · producer · mix engineer

IgoRacks, also known from earlier stages of his career as NIGGAREX and IGOR VESNA, is a hip-hop artist, producer and mix engineer. He has been making rap music since 2007 and works independently across writing, recording, beat production, mixing and visual content.

Contents 1. Career 2. Battle rap 3. Artist names 4. Music and production 5. Selected discography

Career

The project began in the late 2000s with self-recorded rap and independent releases. Over time the work expanded from writing and performing into beatmaking, production, recording and mixing. The current IgoRacks project combines the artist and producer sides of the same practice rather than separating them into different roles.

In addition to finished releases, IgoRacks documents the creative process through videos, studio material, production experiments and personal notes. This website serves as the central archive for releases, current work and that broader creative process.

Battle rap

Before the current IgoRacks period, battle rap was a visible part of the artist's career. Selected appearances and results include:

  • 2010 — Snickers Urbania, 3rd place.
  • 2011 — InDaBattle III, reached Round 4.
  • 2012 — winner of the Crunk/Crank battle.
  • 2014 — Versus Fresh Blood, Season 1; five battles.

Artist names

The artist has released music under several names. NIGGAREX was used during the early rap and battle period. The project later moved to IGOR VESNA, and the current artist name is IgoRacks. The changes mark different stages of the same music career rather than unrelated projects.

Music and production

IgoRacks writes and records vocals, produces beats and mixes his own material. Recent production has focused on minimal hip-hop and trap arrangements, sample-based production, heavy low-end and direct vocal presentation.

Alongside his own releases, he works as a producer and mixing engineer for independent artists. The separate IgoRacks Beats site hosts beat-focused material, while this site acts as the main archive for music, updates and biography.
