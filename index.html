BIOGRAPHY / WIKIPEDIA-STYLE DRAFT · OCTOBER 20, 2026

Igor Vesna

From Wikipedia — working biography mock-up · edition of October 20, 2026

Igor Pavlovich Vesna (born July 21, 1991, Dzhebariki-Khaya, Yakut ASSR) is a Russian rapper, beatmaker, sound engineer, music producer and lyricist. He performed under the pseudonym NI**AREX (2007–2017), later under his own name Igor Vesna. In 2025–2026, he has been developing a new creative project, IgoRacks; the materials also contain the forms IGoRex / iGowRex (the exact spelling and transition date require confirmation).

Contents 1. Biography 2. Musical career 3. New chapter: Thailand and IgoRacks 4. Discography 5. Videography 6. Sources and notes

Biography

He was born on July 21, 1991, in the settlement of Dzhebariki-Khaya in the Yakut ASSR. In 1992, the family moved to the settlement of Shevchenkovo in the Cherkasy region of Ukraine. In 1996, his mother moved with the children to the village of Grishkovka in Altai Krai. Igor graduated from a general education school with a silver medal and from an art school with a red diploma.

At the age of 12, he became interested in music, including the work of Rammstein. As a teenager, he practiced breakdancing, beatboxing and creating instrumentals. In November 2007, he recorded his first track for the qualifying stage of an online rap battle under the name NI**AREX.

Musical career

From the late 2000s to the mid-2010s, he participated in online and offline battles. Notable episodes include third place at Snickers Urbania (2010), participation in InDaBattle III (2011), victory at Crunk/Crank (2012), and performances in the first season of Versus Fresh Blood (2014). In interviews and his own materials, he emphasized his interest in studio music, independent production and satirical rap.

New chapter: Thailand and IgoRacks

In April 2025, Vesna moved to Thailand. During this period, he continued working on his own musical projects, recording, mixing and mastering in a home studio. Under the new stage name IgoRacks (spelling variants in working materials: IGoRex and iGowRex), he is developing the image of an independent DIY artist; the exact date of the adoption of the pseudonym needs to be clarified.

According to the artist's explanation, the name is connected with the name Igor, his earlier pseudonym and the sound of the English word racks (“money,” as well as studio racks — stands for equipment). This ambiguity emphasizes the musician's independent work in a home studio.

After the original album “Grover” (2025), the artist rethought the material: 13 vocal tracks recorded in Thailand in a one-take format formed the basis of a new version of the album. For each vocal track, Vesna independently created a new beat using samples and references to works of classical music; he also independently handled the arrangement, backing vocals, ad-libs, mixing and mastering.

The release of “GROWER: RACKSTAPE VOL. 1,” consisting of 13 tracks, is planned for November 13, 2026 (Friday the 13th). This is a planned date, not an already completed release. The musician talks about the creative process in video blogs, filming and editing the videos himself.
