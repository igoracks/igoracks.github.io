BIOGRAPHY

Independent by design.

IgoRacks is a rapper, beatmaker and mix engineer working across dark trap, minimal hip-hop and experimental rap production.

His background runs from battle rap and independent releases to producing, mixing and mastering his own music.

The current sound is built around hard drums, sparse arrangements, deep low-end and close, direct vocals.
